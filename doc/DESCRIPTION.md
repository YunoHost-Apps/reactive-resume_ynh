Reactive Resume is a free and open-source resume builder that makes creating, updating and sharing your resume easy. Your data stays on your server: no tracking, no ads.

### Features

- Real-time editing with instant preview, many templates and full control over the design (colors, fonts, layout, custom CSS)
- A library of resumes and cover letters, with tags and version history
- Job application tracking (list, board and calendar views)
- Export to PDF, Word (DOCX), Markdown or JSON, and import from JSON Resume, LinkedIn data exports, PDF files or older Reactive Resume versions
- Share a resume through a public link, optionally protected by a password, with view and download statistics
- Built-in checks to see how recruiting software (ATS) reads your resume
- Optional AI assistant, with the provider of your choice (OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama…)
- Many interface languages, light/dark themes, passkeys and two-factor authentication
